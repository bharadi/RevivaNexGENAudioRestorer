1. Overview

Reviva NexGEN Audio Restorer is a native Windows desktop application built in Rust (eframe / egui) that transforms old, noisy, mono, or low-bitrate retro MP3 and audio recordings into clean, wide stereo or 3D binaural studio-mastered tracks—completely offline on your PC.

100% Ready Offline: The Windows installer bundles deepfilternet3.onnx, IRC_1002_C.bin, and a multi-core Rust DSP engine so restoration works immediately without an internet connection.

3. User Manual — Studio Workflow

  1. Import Audio: Drag and drop any .mp3, .wav, .flac, .ogg, .m4a, or .aac file, or click Browse Files from Drive....
  2. Inspect Baseline: View the interactive waveform, spectrogram heatmap, Stereo Channel Badge (2.0 TRUE STEREO vs 1.0 MONO), L/R RMS levels, and detected audio issues.
  3. Configure the 5 Color-Coded Pipeline Blocks:
    • 1. Enhancement Preset (Purple): Choose Clean & Natural, Studio Remaster, 3D Surround, or Vocal Focus.
    • 2. Clean & Denoise (Cyan): Toggle 50Hz/60Hz Hum Notch + Vinyl De-Clicker and Stage 1 Denoise strength.
    • 3. Reconstruct & Spatial (Blue): Adjust Bandwidth Clarity, Stereo Width, and Spatial Mode (Original Stereo, Wide Stereo, or 3D Binaural Surround).
    • 4. Tone & Mixer (Amber): Switch between the 5-Band EQ (60Hz–12kHz) and 4-Stem Mixer (Vocals, Drums, Bass, Other for 4-stem ONNX models).
    • 5. Mastering & Export (Emerald): Toggle R128 (-14 LUFS), Tags (ID3/cover art preservation), GPU (DirectML), and select output format.
  4. Preview or Render: Click Preview 10s for a fast 10-second audition around the playhead, or Reconstruct & Enhance for full-track processing.
  5. Compare & Export: Press Space to Play/Pause and Tab to switch A (Original) ↔ B (Restored). Use Set A / Set B to loop sections, review the side-by-side Quality Score Improvement report, and export your file.

4. Batch Queue, Pluggable Models & Privacy

  • Batch Queue Tab: Add multiple files or an entire folder and click Process All & Export to Folder... to restore an entire collection sequentially.
  • Settings & Models Tab: Select active ONNX model slots, download additional models (HTDemucs v4, BS-RoFormer, DeepFilterNet 3, IRCAM HRIR) with live progress and SHA-256 verification, or plug in custom future .onnx models.
  • About & Legal Tab: Includes the MIT License, Terms of Use, and Zero-Data-Collection Privacy Policy.
